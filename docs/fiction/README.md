## fiction_theory

The Spiral Grid imagines a future in which the old internet has grown into a living network threaded through forests, stone, settlements, and biological perception. Its Concordance infrastructure carries light, heat, and signals through roadside shrines and underground generators. A repair changes both a physical current and the relationships of those who depend on it.

The philosophical foundation is [Spiritual Objectivism](https://leweyg.github.io/spirtual_objectivism/#principles). Reality constrains belief, while a person's access to it remains partial. An intuitive pattern can direct inquiry into what is not yet understood; it becomes a reliable claim only through checking. Virtue means acting consistently with considered values. Institutions and rituals earn their place through the lives and cooperation they sustain, and remain open to revision.

Within this fiction, attunement is a practiced way of noticing possible relationships. Instruments report what they can measure; a felt rhythm may suggest where to look next. Neither an elegant number nor a powerful feeling settles the matter by itself. The Grid can have repeatable resonance without making every interpretation of that resonance true.

The common crisis, the **Drift of Measure**, is a failure of coordination as well as machinery. A relay may work while homes remain dark. A locally efficient allocation may export its costs. A beautiful rite may repeat a mistaken assumption. The player's growing responsibility is to connect observations, promises, and consequences without erasing the differences between them.

## fiction_layers

The current [site correspondences](https://leweyg.github.io/spirtual_objectivism/#correspondences) define the philosophical mapping below. The story travels upward from concrete action toward integration; the source site's explanatory table also reads downward from Bliss toward Body. These are complementary directions through the same layers.

| Layer | Principle | Dramatic expression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body | Breath as your next prayer | A deliberate touch, a walk through a cave, a part fitted where it belongs. |
| Speech | Humanity as political religion | Testimony and shared language make cooperation possible; public categories can be revised. |
| Mind | Thought projects chaos through archetypes toward reason | A suggested pattern is tested, including the possibility that it fails. |
| Wisdom | Beauty in virtue-value alignment | A decision makes its priorities and effects on other people visible. |
| Bliss | God as objective multidimensional reality | Several partial accounts enter a shared view that remains answerable to reality. |

Earlier repository design notes assigned some principles to different layers. Use this mapping for new explanatory prose; retain existing layer and mission IDs so editorial alignment does not alter saved progress. Temple World's architectural and software correspondences are a wider design context, not an implemented feature list for this RPG.

## fiction_archetypes

An archetype supplies a character's recurring question, not their entire personality. Every principal needs something concrete to care for, a strength that helps, and a limitation that another person can expose.

| Pair | Institutional expression | Less institutional expression | Shared question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spirit | Antipions cultivate ritual, calibration, and careful naming. | Yetis read weather, tracks, breath, and changing conditions. | How can disciplined knowledge stay receptive to experience? |
| Exchange | Boarmen keep stores, contracts, and public accounts. | Kobolds work claims, salvage, tunnels, and uncertain yields. | When does accumulation support circulation, and who bears its risks? |
| Social life | Humans mediate among civic interests and competing vocabularies. | The Greek Horde gives appetite, fear, wonder, and boundaries a labyrinthine form. | How can shared constraints preserve different meaningful ways of living? |

“Wild” in the older notes means less mediated by settled institutions. It does not mean morally inferior. These cultures all reason, intuit, cooperate, and make mistakes. Greek Horde is the game's working fantasy label, not a claim about historical Greek people; `AnatipionCatchers` remains the legacy spelling in Antipion IDs.

The site's [fiction](https://leweyg.github.io/spirtual_objectivism/#fiction) offers three broad landscape archetypes: lowland systems favor measurable efficiency; frontier places require integration; distant sanctuaries expose the risks of untested certainty. They guide mood and conflict. They are not additional named regions or a replacement for the frozen game's geography.

The opening gives these tensions personal faces. Orro's inherited language helps him attend to a measurable pulse. Marn's care takes the form of an account whose omissions Pella can name. Talla asks whether all the users of a common supply can remain visible. Varo protects repeatability; Nemi offers a symbol that can be checked; Saima makes public judgment responsible for both evidence and consequences. Their actions unfold in [story_plot](https://github.com/leweyg/jclassicrpg/blob/main/docs/story/README.md#story_plot).

## fiction_world

Each cultural capital extends the same crisis through a different responsibility. These are settings for further stories, not required stops in the Concordance opening.

| Capital | Conflict | Principal cast |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trawamprahshoaw, Common Forum | Civic agreement must acknowledge incompatible needs and vocabularies. | Mara, Oren, Iris |
| Awshowam, Grand Exchange | Stored prosperity must circulate without hiding its costs. | Garrum, Bela, Tusk |
| Migbushoprahshotra, Measured Oasis | A public measure must make room for evidence outside its prediction. | Saima, Varo, Nemi |
| Prahshoshosho, White-Breath Hearth | A durable account must remain attentive to weather and migration. | Aro, Ketu, Seli |
| Deep Ledger | Shared underground work needs trustworthy claims and attention to physical risk. | Vek, Nix, Rul |
| Awshoshotra, Labyrinth of Many Voices | Multiple meaningful paths must coexist within real constraints. | Iris-of-the-Turn, Ember-Tusk, Clasp-of-Seven |

Wamwammigtraawsho, Talla's regional town, is distinct from Awshowam, the Boarman capital. The Measured Oasis is the opening journey's central destination. Names and geography originate in the recovered world where available; principal personalities, capital roles, and the Concordance plot are authored additions. Detailed cultural sketches remain in [fiction_notes](https://github.com/leweyg/jclassicrpg/blob/main/docs/older/v1/root/jclassicrpg-interactions-missions-and-quests.md#capital-stories-and-principal-characters).
