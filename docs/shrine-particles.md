# Shrine particles

`js/world/shrine_particles.js` supplies one shared, bounded alpha-textured quad batch for catalogued surface shrines, including unloaded ones. It uses no lights, bloom, timers, or independent animation loop. Depth testing, depth writing, fog and tone mapping are disabled. Quads are sorted globally back to front using camera-space depth, so turning the camera preserves transparency ordering.

The nearby effect is a slender gold/blue double helix with 3.5 turns, centered above the hut’s table and aligned with the chimney at local X/Z zero, between Y .52 and 1.00. The table is centered during asset baking; the coil radius is 61% of the chimney’s .409088-unit inner radius. Its soft streaks fit between the table top at Y .463 and chimney underside at Y 1.056. Dormant coils fade into view on approach; activated coils glow. A second, finer double helix shares the same center and draw batch: radius .7, height 6, extending from Y −1.5 to Y 4.5, through the ground and above the roof. Its 160 samples per strand reduce to 80 at distance; slight distance scaling keeps the streaks legible. Beyond 30 world units, sparse fixed coils replace the detail. Markers fade out by 380 units, with at most 24 nearest shrines included. Dormant nearby coils use .48 opacity versus .95 when activated. Within 3 units of an activated shrine, distant activated markers rise from .32 to .9 opacity, while dormant hints rise only from .045 to .12; this guidance fades out by 12 units. Route frontiers receive a small .035 emphasis. The outer beacon retains a 1.5 CSS-pixel minimum full streak width to avoid disappearing at distance, without enlarging its coil radius. These visibility settings are configurable in `SHRINE_PARTICLE_DEFAULTS`. Underground views hide the batch. Catalog coordinates wrap with the 1600-unit world.

`SHRINE_PARTICLE_DEFAULTS` defines proportions, range, density and appearance. Pass overrides to `new ShrineParticles(scene, options)` or edit `bakedView.shrineParticles.options` and request a render. `direction: null` (the default) aligns each streak with the exact tangent of its tapered helix. Supply a world-space three-component vector to override the tangent; its projected magnitude controls stretching. `[0, 0, 0]` produces round points. `width` and `streakLength` control particle dimensions. The `beacon` options independently set the outer helix’s height, base, radius, loops, sample count and particle dimensions.

Animation phase uses accumulated active-update seconds from the renderer. `rotationHz: .125` gives one clockwise revolution every eight seconds, viewed from above. Every requested frame advances the clock, including mouse-look and scroll updates. Gaps over 100 ms (idle or suspended frames) contribute only 5 ms, then normal elapsed-time rotation resumes. Hidden tabs and stopped rendering do not advance it. No animation frames are requested solely for the effect. Camera distance does not change the near coil’s phase. Far markers stay fixed. This clock is independent of the game's placeholder discrete turn counter.

Validation: `node --test jCRPG-engine/js/tests/shrine_particles.test.mjs` covers active-time phase and the eight-second rotation period, fixed far particles, buffer reuse, rotation sorting, activation, trail enhancement, cave visibility, world wrapping, capacity, zero-direction points, guidance brightness hierarchy, and distant screen-space width. A local Chrome check verified dormant/active rendering at the opening shrine, no JavaScript exceptions, and a null pending animation frame at idle.
