## story_plot

A narrative treatment of `story:concordance:opening`, including its ending. The linked people, places, and objects resolve to stable IDs in [story_ids](https://github.com/leweyg/jclassicrpg/blob/main/story/ids/README.md); chapter links resolve to the compiled missions. This prose supplies scene narration and connective passages for adaptation into play. The current dialogue scripts remain the source of shipped lines and choices.

### Road

Long before you reached this road, the network had learned to leave its screens. It passed into the stone under the settlements, the trees along their edges, and the instruments with which people listened. They called it the Spiral Grid. Its lights were familiar enough that a dark one could be overlooked until someone needed to come home.

[Orro][orro] waits beside the [roadside shrine][shrine]. He has not mistaken its silence for an answer. His grandmother taught him to pause between pulses; he can measure the pause without claiming to know whether copper breathes. He asks you for one touch.

The shrine answers. For a moment, the problem is small enough to hold: this light was dark, you touched it, and now it keeps a pulse. Orro sends you to [Wammigmig][wammigmig] with that observation.

[Marn][marn] listens before entering it in his account. He knows what supplies are stored and which lamps should draw from them. The working road light belongs in the record, but it cannot tell him whether every home receives the current. [Pella][pella] has been hearing from those homes. When you confirm the repair, he asks you to hear her too.

### Current

Pella knows the difference between a lamp listed as supplied and a room that stays dark. The returning pulse has failed, and the empty [socket][coil_socket] beside her gives that failure a place you can touch. A listening coil will let her check what comes back.

You leave the town for the [southern cave][cave]. The open road narrows to passages; a destination that seemed close above ground must be reached one turn at a time. In the marked chest lies the [coil][coil]. You take it and follow an exit back toward daylight, carrying something more useful than an explanation.

At Pella's socket, the wound piece seats into place. She checks the answer, then checks it again. The pulse holds. It does not settle every household's need, but it gives you a current that can be followed. She asks you to carry that reading to [Talla][talla] in [Wamwammigtraawsho][regional_town], where three branches draw on the same supply.

Orro can help you think about the repair; Marn still has other accounts that need attention. Their work remains beside the road ahead. You can return to it without losing Pella's question.

[story_current](https://github.com/leweyg/jclassicrpg/blob/main/story/ids/README.md#current)

### Branches

Talla Three-Wicks names the branches before asking you to wake them: Exchange, Homes, Labyrinth. Each name belongs to someone who depends on the current. A bright exchange cannot answer for a dark home, and the labyrinth is part of the town's life too.

You awaken the [three conductors][branches_puzzle]. There is no secret order. Each must hold its part of the light, and all must remain awake together. When you return, Talla places the result beside Pella's returning pulse and Orro's roadside observation.

The account has grown. That is precisely why Talla refuses to turn it into a grand theory. At the [Measured Oasis][oasis], a pulse has fallen outside the public prediction. [Saima][saima] will want to know how your readings were made. If hers disagree, Talla asks you to carry the disagreement with equal care.

[story_branches](https://github.com/leweyg/jclassicrpg/blob/main/story/ids/README.md#branches)

### Measures

Saima of Nine Measures receives you among the records by which other people plan their work. She is courteous, and she does not yet trust the reach of your account. A few repaired relays cannot certify a network. A correct instrument can still accompany a harmful decision about whom its current should serve.

[Varo][varo] helps you read the [local channels][balance_puzzle]: Measured, Repeated, Remainder. You inspect before you alter them, set their loads, and watch whether the result holds. The excluded pulse must remain available for comparison; the table cannot become true by removing what troubles it.

When you bring the local balance back, Saima has a reason to listen further. She asks for four independent accounts beyond the oasis. Their keepers will need to receive your reports themselves. The road outward is a test of whether your practice can travel, not simply whether your confidence can.

[story_meaning](https://github.com/leweyg/jclassicrpg/blob/main/story/ids/README.md#meaning)

### Accounts

At [Migawsho][body_town], [Iva][iva] has a spring reading the capital would rather call an instrument error. [Ketu-of-the-Spring][ketu] remembers the thaw, the tracks, and the time together. You visit the reading and hear the witness where the conditions can be understood. One [relay][body_puzzle] is enough for this test. Back with Iva, you record what can be repeated and the conditions under which it happened.

In [Awbuawprahtra][speech_town], [Mira][mira] wants a public record that does not turn sincerity into proof. [Seli][seli] tells you about families arriving before the predicted hour. You hear her account without improving it into a certainty she never offered. Three [lamps][speech_puzzle] keep Observed, Reported, and Interpreted visible together. When you return to Mira, none of the three needs to extinguish another to be heard.

At [Shoprahaw][mind_town], [Varis][varis] has made a beautiful prediction that failed. [Aro][aro] noticed a changing interval in the wind and thaw; his impression gives you something to investigate. The delayed pulse measures six where the old model expected five. You compare the [branches][mind_puzzle], choosing the ones whose combined load meets the observation. Varis receives a corrected result with the failed prediction still beside it. His work can continue because its mistake has become legible.

In [Trashobu][wisdom_town], [Tavi][tavi] has made room for uncertainty for so long that a needed decision has waited with it. [Naru][naru] hears a low sound before a surge. The damaged listener cannot yet test the interval. You follow the marked cave route, retrieve the [listening element][listener], and return to fit it in [Tavi's socket][listener_socket]. The restored device makes the earlier claim available to repeated observation. Tavi can act provisionally, name what remains uncertain, and receive your report without postponing every promise until knowledge is complete.

You may reach these keepers in another order. Each return closes a local responsibility; together their reports give the oasis something no single journey could have supplied.

### Return

Saima now receives four accounts whose differences have survived the road. [Nemi][nemi] names an order for remembering how you reached this point: Ground, Word, Pattern, Welcome. The names connect an observation to a testimony, a tested model, and a place left open for the next person.

At the [four-measure device][final_puzzle], you hear the order before performing it. If you mistake a step, the sequence can begin again; the spring, the witnesses, and the repaired listener do not disappear with your error. When the pattern holds, you return to Saima to decide what the shared record can promise.

The network takes up the completed account. Its public practice now carries Orro's light, Pella's returning current, Talla's branches, and the regional reports together. Nothing in that agreement makes a later observation unwelcome. The road remains open, with other settlements still making their own demands on the light. Your next step can begin from what you have learned, without pretending that the world has finished answering.

## story_style

Use close second-person narration for travel and scene transitions, present-tense action for playable moments, and each character's own vocabulary for speech. Let a physical event precede its interpretation: a light holds, a room remains dark, a prediction misses a pulse. A bridge explains why the next person or place matters, then releases the player to act. The new connective imagery above is narrative treatment; it does not imply additional environmental simulations.

The rhythm expands from person to household, town, and network. The first repair asks for one action and gives a quick visible answer. The cave adds distance and a tangible return. Talla adds simultaneous responsibilities. The capital introduces scrutiny, then independent regional experiences, before a sequence gathers their meaning. Keep quiet moments after a result so gratitude, doubt, or disagreement can register before the next instruction.

Characters are capable people with limitations, rather than lecturers or mouthpieces. Marn's accounting is a form of care; Pella gives it missing information. Saima's caution is earned by the cost of public mistakes. Nemi's symbolism proposes a practice that accepts correction. Show warmth through attention to specific work, and allow disagreement without making either reason or spiritual attention the villain.

## story_binding

Each chapter's prose follows the authored mission's actions and return. The companion [story_ids](https://github.com/leweyg/jclassicrpg/blob/main/story/ids/README.md) binds its names to actual actor, town, item, fitting, puzzle, and mission IDs. Use these IDs in dialogue navigation and objective targets; a renamed character or rewritten paragraph must not acquire a new save identity.

Keep narration, spoken captions, and objective instructions separate when adapting this treatment. Split a scene at the player's next action. Select pending, ready, and completed dialogue from mission state; do not narrate a success before its transaction. The final paragraphs describe the completed path, not a belief every player must profess. Commitments remain explicit choices in dialogue.

On a first visit, a new town or cave carries both mission and exploration. A return carries the changed result: the fitted coil, the revised record, the person now willing to trust you. Count travel through actual corridors and portals when tuning pacing. A scene bridge must not silently teleport the player or reveal an unknown goal; use the known entrance or informant until the target is discoverable.

Body, Speech, Mind, and Wisdom above form one readable telling, but their regional missions can be completed in any order. Four reported completions, Nemi's clue, and the final device gate the synthesis. Some required tasks retain `kind: "side"` in current data; [game_mission_types](https://github.com/leweyg/jclassicrpg/blob/main/game/README.md#game_mission_types) explains that distinction so an adapter does not accidentally make the ending optional.

For shipped captions and state predicates, see [story_opening](https://github.com/leweyg/jclassicrpg/blob/main/scripts/concordance_dialogue.mjs) and [story_saima](https://github.com/leweyg/jclassicrpg/blob/main/scripts/saima_dialogue.mjs).
